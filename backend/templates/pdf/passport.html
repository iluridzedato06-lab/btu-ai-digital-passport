ABOUT
THE AI SKILLSDIGITAL PASSPORT

The BTU AI Skills Digital Passport is your personalized and verifiable record of AI competencies developed throughout your university experience.

AI Skill Stamps may be earned through eligible academic courses, workshops, masterclasses, hackathons, intensive programs, guest speaker sessions, and other AI-related learning experiences recognized by BTU.

Participation in the Passport is voluntary. There is no required number or combination of stamps and no predefined pathway. Students select learning opportunities according to their interests, studies, professional objectives, and desired areas of AI competence.

As new competencies are developed and recognized, additional stamps are added to the Passport, progressively creating an individual AI competency profile.

UNDERSTANDING AI SKILLS STAMPS

AI SKILL
FOCUSED LEARNING

Awarded through an eligible focused learning experience, such as a workshop, masterclass, seminar, guest speaker session, or similar short-format activity.

Represents: Development of a defined AI competency.

AI APPLIED SKILL
APPLIED LEARNING

Awarded through an eligible extended practical learning experience, typically lasting two to three days, such as an intensive workshop, bootcamp, hackathon, or challenge.

Represents: Hands-on application of a defined AI competency.

AI ADVANCED SKILL
EXTENDED ACADEMIC LEARNING

Awarded through the successful completion of an eligible semester-long academic course in which the respective AI competency constitutes a defined and assessed component of the course learning outcomes.

Represents: Extended and formally assessed development of an AI competency.

IMPORTANT

The three stamp types represent different learning formats and depths of engagement. They do not constitute a mandatory sequence.

A student does not need to earn an AI Skill before earning an AI Applied Skill or AI Advanced Skill.
